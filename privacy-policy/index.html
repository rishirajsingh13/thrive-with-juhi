Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Thrive with Juhi Parmar ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and maintaining transparency regarding how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit our website at thrivewithjuhi.com or register for our 2-Day LIVE Experience ("From Emotional Survival to Conscious Creation").

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or enroll in our programs, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and location details provided during checkout or registration.

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business and service delivery purposes, including:

4. Payment Security & Third-Party Service Providers

We respect your privacy and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay processes payments in accordance with PCI-DSS compliance standards.

  • Communication Tools: Email and messaging tools used for sending event information and customer support.
  • Analytics & Ads: Services like Google Analytics and Meta Ads to understand traffic patterns and measure ad performance.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user navigation and remember preferences. You can configure your browser to decline cookies, though certain interactive features may be limited as a result.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with legal requirements.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights or ask questions about our data practices, please contact us at support@thrivewithjuhi.com.
