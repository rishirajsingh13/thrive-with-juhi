Return & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview

Thank you for registering for programs with Thrive with Juhi Parmar (thrivewithjuhi.com). We strive to provide high-quality digital experiences, live webinars, and coaching workshops designed to empower women.

2. Live Digital Events & Webinar Registrations

Due to the live, interactive, and limited-capacity nature of our 2-Day LIVE Webinar Experience ("From Emotional Survival to Conscious Creation"), all ticket registrations (₹499) are non-refundable and non-cancellable once completed.

3. Seat Transfers

While ticket fees are non-refundable, if you are unable to attend the scheduled live session due to an emergency, you may transfer your registration seat to another person. To request a seat transfer, please email us at support@thrivewithjuhi.com at least 24 hours prior to the start of Day 1 with the replacement attendee's full name, email address, and phone number.

4. Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions

In the event that you are charged multiple times for a single registration due to a technical error during payment processing, we will gladly issue a full refund for the duplicate transaction. Please contact support@thrivewithjuhi.com within 48 hours of the transaction with proof of payment (Razorpay payment ID or bank transaction receipt).

5. Refund Processing Timeline

For eligible refund requests (such as duplicate payments or event cancellations initiated by us):

  • Refunds will be processed back to your original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI, or Wallet via Razorpay).
  • Once initiated, refunds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or payment issuer.

6. Event Rescheduling or Cancellation by Host

In the unlikely event that a live session or webinar is cancelled or postponed by Thrive with Juhi Parmar due to unavoidable circumstances, registered participants will be offered:

  • Automatic transfer to the rescheduled event date, OR
  • A full 100% refund of the registration fee.
