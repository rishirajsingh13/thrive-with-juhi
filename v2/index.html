Do You Recognise Any Of This?

Look closely at an ordinary week. Most women are carrying something quietly.

01

Overthinking that leaves you mentally exhausted

Conversations replayed at midnight. Decisions made, then unmade, then made again.

02

Carrying everything alone while appearing entirely capable

The one everyone relies on. The one who absorbs the stress so no one else has to.

03

Guilt that arrives the moment you choose yourself

Saying yes when every part of you needed to say no. Apologising for resting.

04

Feeling invisible inside relationships where you give the most

Loving deeply, showing up consistently - yet feeling that the depth you offer is rarely returned to you.

05

And beneath it all: a quiet grief for the woman you used to be

The one who laughed freely. The one who had dreams that weren't just about keeping life functioning. The one you keep promising you'll get back to.

06

A quiet, persistent anger you rarely let anyone see

Not because you're an angry person - but because you've been swallowing your needs for so long that resentment has begun to settle in.

07

A relationship with your body that feels disconnected

Fatigue that sleep doesn't fix. Tension in your shoulders that never fully leaves. The sense that you've been living entirely in your head.

08

Second-guessing your own voice

Softening your opinions so they ask less of the room. Wondering if you're being "too sensitive" or "expecting too much."

09

An underlying sense of not being enough

No matter how much you achieve, contribute, or manage - there is a quiet whisper that asks whether any of it is truly sufficient.

10

Fear of what happens if you stop

If you don't hold it together, who will? So you keep going, long past the point where your body and mind asked you to rest.

There is a name for this.

Emotional Survival

And most women have never been given a name for what they are experiencing.

The way out isn't more trying.
It's EMOTIONAL SAFETY.

The foundation for coming back to yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 I've never done anything like this before. Is it suitable for beginners? +
Yes, completely. You don't need any prior knowledge of coaching, EFT, or emotional work. Juhi guides every process step-by-step in clear, accessible language.
02 Will I be expected to share personal things in front of the group? +
No. You can participate entirely at your own comfort level. The EFT Tapping release and reflection processes can be done privately, in your own space, with no requirement to speak or share.
03 What is EFT Tapping? +
Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) is a simple, gentle acupressure-based practice that combines gentle tapping on specific meridian points with emotional focus. It is widely used to calm the nervous system, reduce stress, and release stored emotional patterns.
04 What happens if I miss a session? Will there be recordings? +
No recordings will be available. This experience is designed to be lived in real time. Please ensure you can attend both days live on Zoom before registering.
05 How is this different from therapy or counselling? +
This experience is coaching and education focused on emotional awareness, self-regulation, and self-leadership. It is not clinical psychotherapy or psychiatric care. Many women find it complements therapy beautifully; others use it as a powerful standalone self-discovery experience.
06 I'm a housewife / working professional / mother - is this for someone in my situation? +
Emotional survival shows up across every walk of life. The patterns we explore - overthinking, carrying too much, guilt around choosing yourself, disconnection from your needs - are shared by women regardless of career, marital status, or background.
07 What do I need to attend? +
A private, quiet space where you won't be interrupted, a device with Zoom installed, a stable internet connection, and a notebook and pen.
08 What language will the sessions be in? +
The sessions are conducted in a warm, relatable blend of English and Hindi (Hinglish), ensuring everyone feels comfortable and included.
09 Can I attend if I'm not in India? +
Yes, the sessions are live on Zoom and women join from all over the world. Please check the session times in your local timezone.
10 What is the refund policy? +
Due to the limited seats and the nature of live digital events, registrations are non-refundable. If an emergency arises, you may transfer your seat to another woman by contacting us at least 24 hours before Day 1.
